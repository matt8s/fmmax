# References

The documentation and API reference use abbreviated labels for the following sources.

- **[1999 Whittaker]** D. M. Whittaker and I. S. Culshaw, [“Scattering-matrix treatment of patterned multilayer photonic structures,”](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.60.2610) *Physical Review B* **60**, 2610 (1999).
- **[2012 Liu]** V. Liu and S. Fan, [“S4: A free electromagnetic solver for layered periodic structures,”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2012.04.026) *Computer Physics Communications* **183**, 2233–2244 (2012).
- **[2020 Jin]** W. Jin, W. Li, M. Orenstein, and S. Fan, [“Inverse design of lightweight broadband reflector for relativistic lightsail propulsion,”](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsphotonics.0c00768) *ACS Photonics* **7**, 2350–2355 (2020).
- **[2022 Lopez-Fraguas]** E. López-Fraguas, F. Binkowski, S. Burger, S. Hagedorn, B. García-Cámara, R. Vergaz, C. Becker, and P. Manley, [“Tripling the light extraction efficiency of a deep ultraviolet LED using a nanostructured p-contact,”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-15499-7) *Scientific Reports* **12**, 11480 (2022).
- **[2019 Boeddeker]** C. Boeddeker, P. Hanebrink, L. Drude, J. Heymann, and R. Haeb-Umbach, [“On the computation of complex-valued gradients with application to statistically optimum beamforming,”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00392) arXiv:1701.00392 (2017; revised 2019).
- **Grating-method chapters:** E. Popov, ed., [*Gratings: Theory and Numeric Applications*, second revisited edition](https://www.fresnel.fr/files/gratings/Second-Edition/) (2014). The analytic-material and exact-modal pages link directly to chapters 13 and 10.

For the FMMAX paper and BibTeX entry, see [Citing FMMAX](https://github.com/matt8s/fmmax#citing-fmmax).
